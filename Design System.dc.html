04 — Borders & Radius
2px — structure
Section rules, card frames, image frames.
1px — subdivision
Rows inside a section, table lines.
0px radius, always
No rounding anywhere. Accent border marks focus and active fields.
05 — Buttons & Links
Underline link
48px tall, labels flush left in wide buttons, no icon unless it carries meaning. Hover moves one ramp step darker; focus is the 2px accent ring at 2px offset. Text links use a 2px rule under the label that goes accent on hover — never a colour-only change.

08 — Icons
Lucide, 22px, 1.75 stroke, currentColor. Icons appear only where a word would be slower — never as decoration beside a heading.
09 — Motion
Entrance — 12px rise + fade, 700ms, cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1), staggered 60ms. Once per element, on first view only.
State — 150ms on hover and focus. Colour and rule weight only; nothing moves.
Loading — a single red rule filling left to right, then a 260ms cut to the page. No spinner.
Never — parallax, scroll-jacking, counters, letter-by-letter reveals, anything that repeats on scroll up. prefers-reduced-motion disables all of it.
